Our Story

Sports bar, redefined. Comfort, elevated.

We opened The Ivy because Lakeview needed a room that does both — first pitch on every screen, and a kitchen that actually earns your order.

A neighborhood bar & kitchen where the Chicago Tavern pizza is thin and cut in squares, the burgers are smashed to order, and the person pouring your Old Fashioned actually knows what's in it. We're three blocks from the ivy on Waveland, and we borrowed the name honestly.

Whether you're in for a pint before first pitch or booking the back room for a rehearsal dinner, we run this place the way a neighborhood spot should be run: good food, a real bar, and a host who remembers your name by the second visit.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do you take reservations?

We hold a mix of reservations and walk-in seating at the bar and in the dining room. Request a table above or call us at (773) 799-8160 — recommended for parties of six or more and game-day visits.

Is there parking near the restaurant?

Street parking is available along Irving Park Rd and the surrounding side streets. On Cubs game days, we recommend budgeting extra time or using rideshare.

Do you have gluten-free or vegetarian options?

Yes — a dedicated gluten-free beer selection, a Beyond patty swap on any burger, and vegetarian-friendly items across the menu including our White Out pizza and House Salad.

Can kids eat here?

Absolutely. We have a full kids menu for guests twelve and under, plus activity pages to keep them busy while the food comes out.

How do I book a private event?

Head to our Private Events page and fill out the inquiry form — our events team follows up within one business day with availability and pricing.
