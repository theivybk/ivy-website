Scotch

The rules
Made in Scotland, aged at least 3 years in oak. "Single malt" means one distillery, 100% malted barley. "Blended" mixes malt and grain whisky.
Regions, in one word
Speyside: fruity. Highland: rounded. Islay: smoky.
Peat
Barley dried over peat smoke. It tastes like a campfire, bandages or the sea. Always warn a guest before pouring Ardbeg or Lagavulin.
Cask words
Sherry cask: dried fruit, raisin, nuts. Bourbon cask: vanilla, honey.

Bourbon & Rye

The rules
Bourbon: made in the US, at least 51% corn, aged in new charred oak, at least 80 proof. "Bottled in bond" means one distillery, one season, 4+ years, exactly 100 proof.
Wheated
Wheat in place of rye makes it softer and sweeter. All our Weller and Maker's Mark are wheated.
Rye
At least 51% rye. Drier and spicier than bourbon: pepper, clove, mint. The Manhattan whiskey.
Proof
Proof is ABV doubled. Above 100 proof, offer a few drops of water or one big rock.

Tequila

The rules
Made from blue Weber agave, mostly in Jalisco. Every tequila we pour is 100% agave.
Blanco
Unaged or under 2 months. Bright agave, citrus, pepper. The truest taste of the plant.
Reposado
"Rested" 2 to 12 months in oak. Agave plus vanilla and caramel.
Añejo
Aged 1 to 3 years. Sip it like whiskey. A good pick for bourbon drinkers.

2 oz cocktail pour available as a Margarita or Paloma, add $4.

Pouring and talking

  • Ask before you suggest. "Do you like it sweet, spicy or smoky?" One answer narrows the shelf to two bottles.
  • Neat, a few drops of water or one big rock. Water opens up high-proof whiskey like the Weller Full Proof or Rare Breed.
  • Offer a taste of Ardbeg, Lagavulin or anything barrel proof before pouring a full measure.
  • Use the guest's words back. If they say "smooth", reply with "smooth", not "wheated" or "low proof".
  • Reposado and añejo are for sipping. Serve them in a rocks or tasting glass, not as a shot.
  • Upsell one step at a time: Buffalo Trace to Eagle Rare. Weller Special Reserve to Antique 107. Glenfiddich to Balvenie. Don Julio Blanco to Siete Leguas Añejo.
